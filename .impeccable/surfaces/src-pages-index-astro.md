# Home page (EN + Tamil)

Scope: the public single-page site, `src/pages/index.astro` (English) and `src/pages/ta/index.astro` (Tamil). Mode: Persuade with an Operate core (the catalogue and cart).
Audience and job: Salem homeowners and electricians pick products, send the list on WhatsApp for availability and a price quote, then pay and collect at the shop.
Constraints: demo catalogue (Fybros, GM, Philips ranges) until the client sends theirs; no prices shown; the Philips gift offer stays off the site.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Diwali night storefront where the shop's own lights do the decorating and every product is one tap from a WhatsApp quote; it refuses the plain white catalogue.
OWN-WORLD: plum night surfaces, cream text, diya gold as the only action colour, one festive tone per category, Baloo Thambi 2 headings with Hind Madurai body, toran bulbs, rangoli and diyas as the seasonal layer.
STORY: the visitor sees the lit 3D logo, adds items to a list, sends it on WhatsApp, gets a quote, visits to pay and collect.
