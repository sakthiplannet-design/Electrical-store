## Platform

## Stack

Delegated: Astro with static output. The catalogue-and-enquiry model needs no server, cart or payments, so a static site is enough. Astro builds every product page from one data file, has built-in i18n routing for the second language, ships little JavaScript so pages load fast on phones, and deploys free to any static host. Deploy target not chosen yet.

## Users

Two audiences, equally important:

- **Homeowners and DIY buyers** buying switches, wiring, lighting, fans and fittings for a home or small shop. They often don't know exactly which product or size they need and want help choosing.
- **Electricians and trade buyers** who buy in bulk and reorder often. They know what they want and need price, brand and availability quickly.

## Product Purpose

A product catalogue with prices for a local electrical store. Customers browse the catalogue, then order or ask questions by WhatsApp or phone, or come to the store. There is no online cart, checkout or payment. Success means more WhatsApp messages, calls and store visits from people who found the right product on the site.

## Positioning

The store's claim, as stated by the owner, combines four things: trade prices, a wide stock of brands, expert advice on choosing the right product, and quick local delivery. Few shops offer all four together to both homeowners and trade buyers. The site should make each claim visible through real details (prices, brands, a way to ask for advice, delivery terms) rather than slogans.

## Product Principles

1. **Serve both buyers on every page.** Homeowners need plain guidance; trade buyers need a fast scan of price, brand and availability. A product view should give both without making either wade through the other's content.
2. **Every product is one tap from an enquiry.** WhatsApp, with the product already named, or a phone call. The site's job ends when the conversation starts.
3. **Real details over claims.** Show actual prices, brands and delivery terms. When a price is unknown, say "ask for price" rather than guess.
4. **Advice is part of the product.** Make "which one do I need?" easy to ask, because expert help is part of what the store sells.
5. **Two languages, both first-class.** Neither language is an afterthought, including product names, navigation and enquiry messages.
