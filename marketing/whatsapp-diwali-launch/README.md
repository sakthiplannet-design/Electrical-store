# WhatsApp ad: Diwali website launch

- `whatsapp-diwali-launch-ad.png`: the ad, 1080 × 1350 (shows large in chats; also fine as a Status).
- `caption.txt`: the message to send with it, in English and Tamil.
- `ad.html` and `qr.svg`: the source. The QR code opens https://sri-ambica-electricals.web.app/.

To re-render after an edit, serve the repo root (for example `python3 -m http.server`), open
`marketing/whatsapp-diwali-launch/ad.html` at a 1080 × 1350 viewport, and screenshot it.
The ad makes no price or discount claims; add any offer only once the shop confirms it.
